## Why the remake is here?

Well, this repo originates from my love for Kotlin and this mod. 

I met it when I was playing a modpack created by [@Eugene](https://modrinth.com/user/Eugene).

I fell in love with this mod since then. You know, a throwable weapon will bring quantity of fun into game.

Like kill iron golem by attract them with a stick, throw your pickaxe to break a diamond ore in distance

All of these improvements are what attracts me to continue this mod's life.

What's more, if you like it, you can click a star for the [original repo](https://github.com/dainxt/weaponthrow), thank you very much!
